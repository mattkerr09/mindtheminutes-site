What Michigan's law says minutes must show

The law asks forWhereTemplate section
Date, time and place of the meetingMCL 15.269(1)Meeting details
Members present and members absentMCL 15.269(1)Roll call
Any decisions made at the open meetingMCL 15.269(1)Business
All roll-call votes taken at the meetingMCL 15.269(1)Business and Closed session
The purpose of any closed sessionMCL 15.269(1) and 15.267(1)Closed session
The roll-call vote to call a closed sessionMCL 15.267(1)Closed session
Corrections show both the original entry and the correctionMCL 15.269(1)Approval of the minutes
Separate closed-session minutes, kept by the clerk and not publicMCL 15.267(2)Closed session
Proposed minutes available within 8 business daysMCL 15.269(3)Posting dates
Approved minutes available within 5 business days after approvalMCL 15.269(3)Posting dates
Charter townships: a journal signed by the supervisor and the clerk, with yes and no votes on ordinances and resolutions (or a note that the vote was unanimous)MCL 42.7(6)Signatures and Business

When to post them

Proposed minutes must be available to the public within 8 business days after the meeting.

Approved minutes must be available within 5 business days after the meeting at which they are approved.

Find your exact dates with the free deadline calculator.

Michigan's law does not define "business day".

Minutes of the [Board name] [regular or special] meeting

PROPOSED MINUTES. Not yet approved by the board. When the board approves them, change this line to: Approved on [date].

Meeting details

Date: [date]

Time called to order: [time]

Place: [building and street address]

Called to order by: [name and title]

Roll call

Members present: [names]

Members absent: [names]

Also present: [staff and guests, if your board lists them]

Approval of the agenda

Motion by [name], seconded by [name], to approve the agenda [as presented or as amended]. Vote: [carried or failed].

Approval of the minutes of [date of earlier meeting]

Motion by [name], seconded by [name], to approve the minutes [as presented or as corrected]. Vote: [carried or failed].

Corrections, if any. Show both the original entry and the correction. Original: [text]. Correction: [text].

Public comment

[Summary of comments, if your board records them.]

Business

Item [number]: [subject]

Motion by [name], seconded by [name], to [action].

Vote: [voice vote or roll-call vote].

Roll call, if taken: [member] yes, [member] no, [member] abstain.

Result: [carried or failed], [yes count] to [no count].

Closed session (only if one was held)

Motion by [name], seconded by [name], to meet in closed session for this purpose: [purpose, and the part of the Open Meetings Act that allows it].

Roll-call vote: [member] yes, [member] no. Result: [carried or failed].

Closed session began at [time] and ended at [time].

The clerk keeps separate minutes of the closed session. They are not posted with these minutes.

Adjournment

Meeting adjourned at [time].

Signatures

Respectfully submitted by [name], [Clerk or Secretary]. Date: [date].

Charter townships: signed by [Supervisor] and [Clerk].

Posting dates

Proposed minutes available to the public by: [date, within 8 business days after the meeting].

Approved on: [date]. Approved minutes available by: [date, within 5 business days after that meeting].
