Questions, answered

Questions before your first draft

Is it free?

Your first meeting is free. $79 once per computer after that. No card for the free meeting.

Does my recording get uploaded?

No. Meetings, recordings and drafts stay on your computer. Speech and speaker separation run there. License activation is the app's one network call, only when you press Activate.

Can I use a transcript instead of a recording?

Yes. Choose a recording or a transcript, check the meeting date, then press Make the draft.

What computer do I need?

Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Is the draft ready to approve as it is?

No. It is always a draft for the clerk to check and correct before the board approves it. It is not legal advice.

Does it know Michigan's minutes deadlines?

Yes. Michigan minutes deadline dates are built in. Proposed minutes must be available within 8 business days after the meeting. Approved minutes must be available within 5 business days after the meeting at which they are approved.

What about closed sessions?

The closed-session guard keeps only the purpose and the vote in the open minutes, under MCL 15.269(1). The clerk keeps a separate set of closed-session minutes.
