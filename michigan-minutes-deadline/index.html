      Proposed minutes must be available within 8 business days after the meeting. Approved minutes must be available within 5 business days after the meeting at which they are approved. Michigan's law doesn't define 'business day'. The safe dates count every Monday to Friday, skipping no holidays. Checked against MCL 15.269(3) on legislature.mi.gov, 8 November 2026.
